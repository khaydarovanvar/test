Rules & Regulations

The single source of truth for how STEMCO competitions run — identical in every country.

Subjects & categories

STEMCO runs olympiad-style competitions in:

  • Sciences — Chemistry, Physics, Biology (grades 7–12) and Science (grades 1–8).
  • Mathematics — grades 1–12.
  • English — launching with the 2026 season.

Participants compete in age-band categories (Primary Junior 1–3, Primary Senior 4–6, Secondary Junior 7–9, Secondary Senior 10–12, where applicable) against peers of the same band — and against their own previous result.

Rounds

Qualifying rounds. Qualifying Round 1 and Qualifying Round 2 are conducted online on the STEMCO platform — open-resource, multiple-choice, with a 24-hour submission window (~2 hours recommended). Students must obtain at least 40% in a qualifying round exam to receive an invitation to the Global Final. Country representatives may conduct separate onsite, invigilated national rounds with their own qualifying processes.

Global Final round. Held in Singapore, by invitation only, under supervision with fixed exam durations.

Exam regulations (Global Final)

  • Format. The competition is conducted in a digital format; participants bring their own laptop.
  • Supported devices: MacBook with Safari, or Windows laptop with Chrome. iPads, Android tablets and smartphones are not supported.
  • Internet use is strictly limited to exam-related activities.
  • Calculators are permitted — bring your own personal calculator.
  • Draft work. A notebook and pen are provided for draft use.
  • AI assistants. Chat support on the platform is not available during the exam.
  • Language. The default exam language is English; translations may be available for selected rounds.
Appellation. Participants may report problematic questions directly from the platform after completing the exam.

Scoring

  • Each correct answer earns 1 point.
  • Empty or incorrect answers receive 0 points — there is no penalty for incorrect answers, so attempt every question.
  • The overall score is calculated as a percentage of the maximum.
  • Time management counts toward awards in tie situations.

Awards

Awards are determined by overall ranking. Every participant receives a digital certificate and a personal progress card. Absolute winner awards are presented to the top performer in each grade; ties are resolved by score then completion time. Award discounts apply to future Global Final participation fees: Gold −15%, Silver −10%, Bronze −5%.

Integrity

Exams are closely supervised (with auto-proctoring at the Global Final), and any attempt to cheat results in immediate disqualification. Qualifying rounds use randomised question order and shuffled answer options for every participant.
