02

Rounds

Two chances to qualify

→

Qualifying Rounds

Qualifying Round 1 and 2 run online on the STEMCO platform — open-resource, multiple choice, 24-hour window (~2 hours recommended). Results arrive just 2 days after the exam. Country representatives may run onsite, invigilated national rounds with their own criteria.

→

Score 40%+

Score at least 40% in a qualifying round to earn your invitation. All gold, silver and bronze award winners of the Qualifying Rounds are invited to the Global Final.

Global Final · Singapore

By invitation only, supervised, with fixed exam durations. Explore the Global Final →

03

Competition format

Made to fit every classroom

💻

Online — or on paper

Online on a laptop from any device with a web browser. Paper exams are available — partners inform STEMCO in advance so papers arrive on time.

⏱

Asynchronous day

Access opens for one full day — start any time. The timer runs from Start to Finish; time management counts toward awards.

📚

School syllabus only

Questions build on the general school syllabus with the Singapore curriculum as the basis. No olympiad coaching needed.

🌍

Your language

English is the default; translations into multiple languages are available on request for qualifying rounds.

04

Exam regulations · Global Final

Exam day, by the book

  • Digital format. Participants bring their own laptop — MacBook with Safari, or Windows laptop with Chrome. iPads, Android tablets and phones are not supported.
  • Internet use is strictly limited to exam-related activity, and platform chat support is off during the exam.
  • Calculators allowed — bring your own. A notebook and pen for draft work are provided.
  • Appellation. Report any problematic question directly from the platform right after finishing.

zero tolerance

Integrity

Exams are closely supervised with auto-proctoring. Any attempt to cheat means immediate disqualification. Qualifying rounds shuffle question order and answer options for every single participant.
