Our story

More than a decade of making learning tangible

STEMCO promotes functional literacy and academic excellence through experience-based academic competitions for K-12 students — with the proven best practices of the Singapore education system as our benchmark for quality, rigor and applied learning.

Healthy competition makes learning intrinsically motivating, tangible and fun. That is what carries us “From Singapore to the World”.

Participation

The road to Singapore

The Global Final Round in Singapore is by invitation only. Here is how you earn yours.

→

Qualifying Rounds

Two chances to qualify — Qualifying Round 1 or Qualifying Round 2, both conducted online on the STEMCO platform. Open-resource, 24-hour window, ~2 hours recommended.

→

Score 40%+

Achieve a minimum score of 40% in a qualifying round examination to earn your invitation. In some countries, representatives run separate onsite, invigilated national rounds.

Global Final · Singapore

Fly to Singapore for the supervised, auto-proctored Global Final — plus an unforgettable academic and cultural program. Explore the Global Final → See you at Changi!

STEMCO 4 Equity: selected students are sponsored based on need and achievement, and accompanying mentors are waived the participation fee. Contact your country representative for your local qualification pathway.
