About the competition

What is STEMCO Physics?

STEMCO Physics is an international Olympiad-style Physics competition within the STEMCO framework. Motion, energy, waves, light — every question starts from the world you can see and asks you to explain it with clear reasoning and the general school syllabus.

Just like in real life

The format

  • Open resources. Online, at home or school — with full access to the internet, notes and videos. Searching helps, but rarely gives the answer away.
  • Your 24 hours. Start any time in the competition window. The clock runs from Start to Finish, and time management counts toward awards.
  • Fair play built in. Randomised question order and shuffled answer options for every single participant.
  • Personal report. A digital certificate and an individual progress card measuring mastery, skills and time management.

Syllabus outline

What it covers

Nothing beyond the general school syllabus, with Cambridge and K-12 cores as a rough guideline.

Mechanics

Motion, forces, energy, momentum and simple machines.

Waves & optics

Sound, light, reflection, refraction and wave behaviour.

Electricity & magnetism

Circuits, current, voltage, resistance and magnetic effects.

Thermal & modern physics

Heat, states of matter and first glimpses beyond the classical world.
