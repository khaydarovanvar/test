About the competition

What is STEMCO English?

STEMCO English is the newest olympiad in the STEMCO framework — an international English competition testing functional literacy: reading with insight, reasoning with language and communicating with precision. Built on the general school syllabus, launching with the 2026 season.

Just like in real life

The format

  • Open resources. Online, at home or school — with full access to the internet, notes and videos. Searching helps, but rarely gives the answer away.
  • Your 24 hours. Start any time in the competition window. The clock runs from Start to Finish, and time management counts toward awards.
  • Fair play built in. Randomised question order and shuffled answer options for every single participant.
  • Personal report. A digital certificate and an individual progress card measuring mastery, skills and time management.

Understanding, interpreting and evaluating a wide range of texts.

Vocabulary in context

Word choice, nuance, idiom and precision.

Grammar & structure

Sentence craft, cohesion and clarity.

Applied communication

Using language to reason, persuade and explain.
