Who gets to come

Strictly by invitation

🏅

Award winners are invited

All gold, silver and bronze award winners from the Qualifying Rounds receive an invitation — open to all grades. Partners can organize national rounds with their own invitation criteria.

👪

Bring your people

Parents and additional guests (7 years and older) are welcome to accompany invited students all the way to Singapore.

🎓

Mentors travel free

Participation is free for one mentor for every 10 participants — students, parents and guests included.

✈️

Two ways to join

The 2-day Olympiad program is the default; the 5-day Inclusive program is offered as a convenience for travelling delegations.

Choose your journey

Two programs, one finals

Olympiad program · 2 days
2 days · for neighboring countries
  • STEMCO competitions
  • STEMCO's Got Talent (SGT) Show
  • University talk & museum visits
  • Opening & award ceremonies
  • Celebratory buffet lunch
  • Goodie bags, medals and certificates
Inclusive program · 5 days
5 days · for distant countries
  • Everything in the Olympiad program, plus
  • Accommodation and transport
  • Visa invitation & support
  • Top attractions and landmarks of Singapore
  • Days to recover from jet lag and adjust to time zones
  • Time to acclimatize to Singapore's weather

Fair competition ground and convenience — that is why delegations from far away choose the Inclusive program. See prices.
